# 00 · Start here

## Who this is for
Startup developers who are new to Azure or just joined
Microsoft for Startups and are activating their credits.

## What you will accomplish
- Understand how Azure is organized for startup workloads
- Activate your Microsoft for Startups credits
- Deploy a first workload or confirm your environment is ready
- Know where to go next based on what you are building

## The path (recommended order)

1. **Create or confirm your Azure account**
   https://azure.microsoft.com/free/

2. **Activate your Microsoft for Startups credits**
   https://foundershub.startups.microsoft.com/
   *Credit amounts and eligibility details live at the official source above.*

3. **Understand the Azure environment basics**
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/azure-fundamentals/

4. **Set up your first resource group and subscription structure**
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ready/azure-setup-guide/organize-resources

5. **Choose your first workload path**
   [01-build-fast](../01-build-fast/) — if you are shipping something now
   [04-ai-agents](../04-ai-agents/) — if you are building an AI product
