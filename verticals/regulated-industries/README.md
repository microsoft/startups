# Regulated industries

Industry-specific resources for startup developers building for healthcare, life sciences, and financial services.

Use these pages alongside the [guided paths](../../paths/) to identify architecture topics and questions to discuss with customers, security teams, and compliance advisers.

| Industry | Topics to explore |
|---|---|

## How to use these resources

1. Start with your workload: identify the data you process, the users you serve, and the regions in which you operate.
2. Review the relevant industry page for context and links to official documentation.
3. Confirm applicable requirements with qualified legal, privacy, security, and compliance specialists.
4. Use the related paths below to plan implementation and prepare evidence for customer assessments.

## Scope and responsibility

These pages are informational starting points, not legal advice, certification checklists, or a determination that a regulation applies to your workload. Requirements depend on your use case, data, jurisdiction, and customer agreements.

Using Azure services or following these resources does not by itself establish regulatory compliance or guarantee a successful customer review. Consult the [Azure compliance documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/compliance/) and relevant specialists to validate service scope, configurations, and responsibilities before production use.
